## Responsivity vs. Wavelength 
- responsivitiy of the photodiode is dependent on the wavelength of the incident light 
- max responsitivity is at 775nm (higher responsitivity with higher wavelength), plateaus after 775nm 
- find the optimum wavelength for chosen photodiode 
- diode current generated by incident light $I_L = r_{phi}*phi_{e}$ (r is diode flux responsivity & $phi_e$ is the radiant flux energy in W)

## Junction Capacitance
- smaller junction capacitance means lower output noise (need to minimize the junction capacitance)
- increasing the reverse bias voltage $V_R$ will decrease the junction capacitance 
- I-V characteristics 
    - diode characteristics shift with applied light 

## Transimpednace Amplifier 
- converts electric current to voltage 
- calculate the output voltage by multiplying the input current (from the photodiode) with the feedback resistor ($R_F$)
- SBW (signal bandwidth) with cutoff frequency ($f_c$) is controlled by $R_f$ & $C_f$
- NBW (noise bandwidth)
- NG (noise gain)
